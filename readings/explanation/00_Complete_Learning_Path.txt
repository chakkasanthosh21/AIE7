📚 COMPLETE TOPIC GUIDE

1. 🎯 PROMPT ENGINEERING
========================
File: 01_Prompt_Engineering_Complete_Guide.txt

What You'll Learn:
• Crafting effective prompts for AI models
• Advanced techniques like few-shot learning
• Prompt templates and best practices
• Real-world applications

Key Concepts:
• Specificity and clarity in prompts
• Context provision and role-based prompting
• Chain of Thought (CoT) reasoning
• Prompt evaluation and optimization

Why It's Important:
Prompt engineering is the foundation of working with AI models. It's how you communicate with AI systems to get the best possible results.

2. 🔍 EMBEDDINGS AND RAG
========================
File: 02_Embeddings_and_RAG_Complete_Guide.txt

What You'll Learn:
• How embeddings represent text as numbers
• Building Retrieval Augmented Generation (RAG) systems
• Vector databases and similarity search
• Advanced RAG techniques

Key Concepts:
• Semantic similarity and vector representations
• Document processing and chunking
• Retrieval strategies and reranking
• RAG evaluation and optimization

Why It's Important:
RAG systems combine AI with external knowledge, making responses more accurate and up-to-date. This is crucial for building reliable AI applications.

3. 🤖 AI AGENTS

File: 03_AI_Agents_Complete_Guide.txt

Key Concepts:

• Learning and adaptation mechanisms

Why It's Important:
AI agents represent the future of AI - systems that can operate autonomously, make decisions, and take actions to achieve goals.
